1. Pure Mathematical Dynamic Distance Control Logic

To eliminate arbitrary visual positioning, circle positions and overlap distances are calculated dynamically using 100% actual and forecast metrics:

  • Broadcasting ⇔ Broadcast-Related (Horizontal Overlap): Driven by the Digital Ratio within Related Segment ($= \frac{\text{Digital Revenue}}{\text{Related Total}}$). As digital streaming (TVer/SVOD) expands over time, the blue and cyan circles merge deeper together.
  • Broadcast-Related ⇔ Non-Broadcasting (Vertical Distance): Driven by the Non-Synergy Ratio ($= \frac{\text{Non-Media Non-Synergy Revenue}}{\text{Non-Broadcasting Total}}$). Broadcasters with large non-media operations (e.g. Fuji Sankei Building real estate, TBS PLAZA retail) have their amber circle automatically pushed downward into an isolated position.
